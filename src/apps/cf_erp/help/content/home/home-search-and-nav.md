How to find any order, item or screen fast, and how the menus are laid out. It is for everyone.

## What you see
- **The top bar**: the sections Home, Sales, Management, Accounts, Purchase, Catalog, Production, Stores, Maintenance and Setup. You see only the sections your role may open. Click a section to open its first screen. Its screens show as a second row of tabs. A few screens sit in two sections — the Dashboard (Management and Production), Order costing (Management and Accounts), and the machine register and Tools (also in Maintenance); it is the same page, and the menu stays on the section you opened it from.
- **Search** (the box with **⌘K**) at the right of the top bar.
- **Create** (the **+** button): New order, New item, Receive stock, New purchase order, New machine, New customer. You see only what you are allowed to create.
- The light/dark switch.
- **Your name**, which opens a menu with **Switch app** and **Sign out**.

## How to search
1. Press **Ctrl K** (on a Mac, **⌘K**), or click **Search**.
2. Type at least two letters, such as an order code, an item code or a name. The box says *Search orders, items, machines, stock — or jump to a screen*.
3. Results come in groups:
   - **Actions**, such as New order, New item, Receive stock, New flow or New customer.
   - **Go to**: screens. Words work too: *gst* finds Company tax details, and *grn* finds Receive against PO.
   - **Records**: orders, items, machines and more.
4. Move with the **↑ ↓** keys and press **Enter**, or click a result. **Esc** closes the box.
5. With the box empty you see **Recent**, the screens you opened last.

## Other keys
- **?** shows the keyboard sheet.
- In lists, **↑ ↓** moves between rows and **Enter** opens the highlighted row.
- In a dialog, **Enter** saves. **Shift Enter** adds a new line in a notes field.

## Good to know
- Most lists remember their filters in the web address, so you can copy the link and send it to a colleague.
- Many boards have a full-screen button for a big screen on the shop floor or in a meeting.
- On a phone, the **☰** button at the top left opens the menu.
