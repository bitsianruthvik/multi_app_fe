The screen on the shared tablet at each machine. The operator taps their name and records what the machine does: jobs started, paused and done, and why it stood still. Everything is a big button — no codes are typed.

## Getting started at a machine
1. **Which machine?** — tap the machine (search, or **Show all machines**). The tablet remembers it.
2. **Who are you?** — tap your name. Tap **Not you?** at the top to change person; **Machines** goes back to the list.

## Now
1. **Up next** lists the jobs waiting for this machine, in planned order. Tap one or more, then **Start** (or **Start N together**).
2. A job marked **Not in the plan** asks once before starting — **Start anyway** or **Back**. **Not ready yet** means it still waits for something.
3. A running job shows its timer with **Pause** and **Done**. **Done** asks **How many finished?** (filled with what is left); **Some scrapped?** adds a scrapped count.
4. A paused job reads "Paused — tap to continue".
5. If the machine stands idle, a banner asks "Machine stopped since … — why?". Tap the reason. You can also tap **Machine stopped?** yourself. Tap **Back to work** when it runs again.

## My day
- The whole day as one bar: shift shaded, work green, overtime blue, stops amber, not recorded grey, with totals **Work**, **Stopped**, **Not recorded**.
- For each gap, tap **It was work** or **It was a stop**. **Add work** and **Add stop** fill in from paper notes or memory. Arrows move to earlier days.

## Raise a fault
Tap **Raise a fault** in the header when the machine breaks down. See [Raise a fault](help:maintenance-raise-fault).

## Good to know
- This screen has no app menu, so the help button may not be on it — a supervisor can open this page from Help.
- Names come from Setup › Operators; stop reasons from Setup › Stop reasons.
- Run hours and tonnes recorded here drive preventive maintenance plans.
