Customer material that is not needed or not accepted. It is theirs, not ours, and not earmarked for any work. Stores uses this list to send it back.

## What you see
- **To return to the customer** — material a customer sent beyond what their order needs, or a substitute the MD did not accept, by customer.
- **Different items the customer sent** — substitutes, each with its status (waiting, approved or rejected).
- "Nothing to return to customers." when the list is clear.

## How to send material back
1. Note the order the material belongs to.
2. Open that order's **Customer material** tab and use **Return**.
3. The return movement takes the row off this list by itself.

## Good to know
- The list is worked out afresh every time you open it — there is nothing to tick off here.
- A customer's material is never valued as ours and never used for another job.
- A substitute plate a customer sent goes to the MD on Plate changes.
