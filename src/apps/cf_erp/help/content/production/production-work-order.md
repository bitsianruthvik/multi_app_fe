The older work order view. A line at the top says so and links to **Open the contractor work order** (Accounts › Work orders), where contractor work orders are run: their contractor PO, progress and payments. This page opens from **Production › Dashboard › Work orders** and from an older work order's contractor page. The old Work orders list is retired (its address opens Accounts › Work orders).

## What you see
- The code, the contractor, its status and the order.
- **Dates and notes** — **Start**, **Due**, **Notes**.
- **What it covers** — the operations handed to this contractor, by piece.

## What you can do
1. Move it on with the one button its state offers: **Issue** (draft → issued to the contractor), **Start** (issued → in progress), **Done** (in progress → done).
2. Change **Start**, **Due** or **Notes** and click **Save**.
3. **Cancel** calls it off.

## Good to know
- New contractor work is given from a production order (**Give work to a contractor**), approved by Management and paid in Accounts.
- Changes here need the production manage permission, and only while the work order is open. The contractor work order page needs the accounts permissions.
