This guide is for the MD and the managers who approve, watch the plant and the money, and decide when plans move. In the app, Management says yes or no; Sales, Purchase, Stores, Production and Accounts do the work.

## Your screens
- **Home**: what waits for you today.
- **Management › Dashboard**: machines, orders and contractors for a period.
- **Management › Plan health**: the plan against its baseline.
- **Management › Approvals**: the one inbox of everything that needs your yes.
- **Management › Order costing**: the margin of each order (the same page as Accounts › Order costing).
- **Accounts › Billing** and **Receipts**: what is billed, owed and paid.

## Every morning
1. Open [Home](help:home-cockpit). Look at **Waiting on you**. **Purchase orders to approve** takes you straight to Approvals. Check **Orders past their date** too.
2. Open [Approvals](help:management-approvals). Work through the **Waiting** cards:
   - **Customer acceptance**: read the charges, milestones and the **Estimated value** with its **margin over material**. **Approve** confirms the order. Billing then starts by itself, and the advance becomes due to bill at once.
   - **Rate change after acceptance**: the yellow **What changes** box says what moved and what it was before.
   - **Purchase order**: each line is compared with the other quotes on its purchase request. Look for *the lowest quote*. Approval places the order with the vendor.
   - **Contractor PO**: work given to a contractor by the tonne. Approval issues the work order.
   - **Close a PO short**: material that will not come, and why.
   - **Re-baseline**: the planner wants to move the committed plan. The card lists each order's new date and the days lost.
   - If something is wrong, click **Send back** and say why. The person who asked reads your reason.
3. Open [Plan health](help:management-overview): what is slipping most, and why.

## Every week
1. Open the [Dashboard](help:management-dashboard) for **This week**.
   - **By order**: late or at risk orders, what blocks them, tonnes made.
   - **By machine**: utilisation, stops by reason, and time not recorded.
   - **Work orders**: how far each contractor is.
2. Open [Order costing](help:management-order-costing). Sort by **Margin**. Click an order to see its cost sheet: charged so far, material, consumables, scrap and offcut credits. Watch the **Not costed** column — a missing scrap rate or plate rate makes margins look wrong.
3. Open [Billing](help:accounts-billing) and look for red **Overdue** cards. Ask Accounts to follow up.

## When you set a rule for the company
- The AI nesting's plate rules (grade and impact class, custom plate sizes, how many plate sizes per thickness) are in [Cutting](help:setup-cutting). BR and BO plates are kept apart by default.
- The scrap rate is on the same page.

## Good to know
- You see only the approval kinds you hold. Each kind is its own permission.
- You cannot approve what you asked for yourself; someone else in Management must. During testing, one company may allow self-approval; that is switched off before going live.
- Approvals never disappear. **Decided** keeps who decided, when and why.
